## 💎 2. PREMIUM AI TOOLS & "DEMO ACCESS MODE" SPECIFICATION

To demonstrate monetization while letting faculty test all paid features without real credit card friction, every premium tool includes a **"Simulated Instant Unlock / Demo Mode"**:

### The 4 Working Premium AI Tools:
1. **AI Business Valuation Engine:** Generates an investor-grade 5-page financial valuation summary (DCF, Asset multiple, and EBITDA multiples).
2. **AI Forensic Audit & Debt Restructuring Tool:** Scans financial inputs for red flags, debt-trap risks, and gives a prioritized restructuring checklist.
3. **AI Automated Contract & NDA Drafter:** Generates customized, legally-formatted Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and Letters of Intent (LOIs) with live editable fields.
4. **100-Day AI Turnaround Roadmap Builder:** Breaks down a company's recovery into a 3-phase milestone execution plan (Days 1–30: Cash stabilization, Days 31–60: Vendor renegotiation, Days 61–100: Growth scaling).
